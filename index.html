What We Do 我们做什么

Founder salons. Small on purpose. 创始人沙龙，刻意保持小规模。

Small, curated, in-person gatherings — founders, investors, and operators around one table. No stage, no pitch night, no name-tag mixer. Small on purpose, so conversations go deep. 小型、精心组织的线下聚会——创始人、投资人和操盘手围坐一桌。没有舞台，没有路演，没有举着名牌的社交酒会。小，是刻意的选择，因为只有小桌子才聊得深。

The Australian venture ecosystem has very little Chinese presence, and founders and investors on both sides want to connect. We're building that room. 澳洲创投圈里华人的声音还很少，而两边的创始人和投资人其实都想认识彼此。我们想把这张桌子搭起来。

Who It's For 适合谁
  • Chinese founders, investors & operators in Australia 在澳洲的华人创始人、投资人与操盘手 Builders who want a room that feels like theirs. 想要一个真正属于自己的圈子。
  • 出海 founders landing in Australia 出海落地澳洲的创始人 Australia is an attractive, under-discussed landing point for Chinese companies going global. 对出海企业来说，澳洲是一个被低估的落脚点。我们懂本地，也懂你从哪里来。
  • Australian VC & startup people 澳洲本地的 VC 与创业者 Local investors and operators who want to meet the Chinese founder community. 想认识华人创业者圈子的本地投资人和从业者。
