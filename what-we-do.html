What We Do 我们做什么

Everything starts at the table. 一切从一张桌子开始。

New Gold Mountain is built on one thing: a network of Chinese founders, investors and operators in Australia that we know personally. Everything we do puts that network to work — for the people in it, for the investors who should meet them, and for partners in China looking for a way in. 新金山的根基只有一样：一个我们亲自认识、亲自筛选过的圈子——在澳洲的华人创始人、投资人和操盘手。我们做的每一件事，都是让这个圈子真正发挥作用：为圈子里的人，为应该认识他们的投资人，也为想走进澳洲的国内伙伴。

一
The community 社群

Founder salons 创始人沙龙

Small, curated gatherings — founders, investors and operators around one long table. Invite-only and bilingual. No stage, no pitch night, no name-tag mixer: small on purpose, so the conversations go deep. 小型、精心组织的线下聚会——创始人、投资人和操盘手围坐一张长桌。邀请制，中英双语。没有舞台，没有路演，没有举着名牌的社交酒会：小，是刻意的选择，因为只有小桌子才聊得深。

This is where trust gets built. Everything else on this page depends on it. 信任在这里建立。这一页上的其他事，都以此为基础。

二
Founders ↔ capital 项目 ↔ 资本

Introductions 投融资对接

We connect good founders with the investors who fit them — angels, Australian VCs, and Chinese capital looking at Australia. Every introduction is warm and considered, in both directions: we only make one when we'd put our name on it. 我们把好的创业者介绍给真正合适的投资人——天使投资人、澳洲本地 VC，以及关注澳洲的中国资本。每一次引荐都是双向的、认真考虑过的：只有我们愿意为之背书，才会牵这根线。

  • For founders — investors who actually back your stage and sector, not a list of names. 对创业者：介绍真正投你这个阶段、这个赛道的投资人，而不是一串名单。
  • For investors and funds — a vetted flow of Chinese-founder startups in Australia, and 出海 companies landing here. 对投资人与机构：经过筛选的项目源——澳洲本地的华人创业公司，以及出海落地澳洲的企业。
  • For companies landing in Australia — the local people you'll need next: investors, customers, advisors. 对出海企业：落地之后需要认识的本地人——投资人、客户、顾问。
三
China ↔ Australia 中国 ↔ 澳洲

Partner events & project curation 合作活动与项目臻选

For institutions, associations and companies from China that want to do something real in Australia — a startup showcase, a sourcing trip, a delegation meeting local founders — we're the partner on the ground. We run the local side end to end and, above all, curate the projects, so your time goes to the ones worth it. 对于想在澳洲真正做点事的国内机构、协会和企业——办一场创业项目展示、来澳考察寻找项目、或带团与本地创业者交流——我们就是你在当地的合作伙伴。本地的事我们全程承接；更重要的是帮你臻选项目，让你的时间花在真正值得的项目上。

  • Showcases and pitch events, co-hosted with local partners 创业项目展示与路演活动，与本地伙伴联合举办
  • Project sourcing and curation — we know the founders, so the shortlist holds up 项目寻源与臻选——创始人我们都亲自认识，推荐的名单经得起推敲
  • Local execution — venue, co-hosts, the right audience, bilingual hosting 本地执行——场地、联合主办方、对的观众、双语主持
  • It works the other way too: Australian organisations that want to reach the Chinese founder community. 反过来也一样：想接触华人创业圈的澳洲机构，也可以找我们。
