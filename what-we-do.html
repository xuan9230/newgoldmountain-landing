What We Do 我们做什么

Everything starts at the table. 一切从一张桌子开始。

New Gold Mountain is built on one thing: a network of Chinese founders, investors and operators in Australia that we know personally. Everything we do puts that network to work — for the people in it, for the investors who should meet them, and for partners in China looking for a way in. 新金山的根基只有一样：一个我们亲自认识、亲自筛选过的圈子——在澳洲的华人创始人、投资人和操盘手。我们做的每一件事，都是让这个圈子真正发挥作用：为圈子里的人，为应该认识他们的投资人，也为想走进澳洲的国内伙伴。

一
Community社群

Founder salons创始人沙龙

Once a month, moving between Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane: founders, investors and operators around one long table. Invite-only, bilingual, small on purpose. 每月一场，在墨尔本、悉尼、布里斯班之间轮换：创始人、投资人和操盘手围坐一张长桌。邀请制，中英双语，刻意保持小规模。

二
Capital资本

Introductions投融资对接

Good founders, introduced to the investors and funds that fit them. For investors, a vetted flow of Chinese-founder and 出海 startups in Australia. Only introductions we'd put our name on. 把好的创业者介绍给真正合适的投资人和机构；为投资人提供经过筛选的项目源——澳洲华人创业公司与出海企业。只做我们愿意背书的引荐。

Already on board已加入 Friends from Blackbird, Startmate, the startup podcast 澳洲创业访谈录 and Hundun Academy (混沌学园), who've agreed to come share what they know and meet the good projects we bring them. 来自 Blackbird、Startmate、澳洲创业访谈录、混沌学园的朋友——他们愿意来分享经验，也愿意对接好的项目。

三
Programs培训

Founder bootcamps创业营

We design and teach founder programs with partners: the curriculum, startup-101 training, picking the strongest projects at the end, and introducing them to investors. 与合作伙伴一起设计和讲授创业课程：课程大纲、创业 101 培训，结营时甄选优秀项目，并推荐给投资机构。

四
Cross-border跨境

China ↔ Australia中澳连接

For institutions and companies from China, the partner on the ground in Australia: showcases, delegations, a curated shortlist of projects. And we work the China end too — fairs, innovation parks, local government, investors. 国内机构和企业在澳洲的落地伙伴：项目展示、考察交流、臻选项目名单。我们也在国内那一端下功夫——投资洽谈会、产业园区、地方政府与投资人。

    CIFIT & the Gulangyu Forum9·8 投洽会 · 鼓浪屿论坛

    Invited to China's biggest two-way investment fair, 26 years running, and the Gulangyu Forum alongside it. A week on the AI, robotics and investment floor, then a day with the district government — through Xiamen's AI innovation park and in front of local investors. What we heard again and again: Chinese companies want customers abroad, and Australia barely registers on their map yet. Gap or opportunity, depending which side you're standing on. 受邀参加中国国际投资贸易洽谈会——国内规模最大的双向投资盛会，已举办 26 年——以及同期的鼓浪屿论坛。一周时间泡在 AI、机器人与投资展区；另用一天随区政府走访厦门人工智能产业园，并与当地投资人面对面交流。反复听到的是：国内企业非常想要海外客户，而澳洲几乎还不在他们的地图上。是空白还是机会，取决于你站在哪一边。
